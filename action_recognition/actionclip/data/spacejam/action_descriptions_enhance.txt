pass:a basketball player using one or both hands to thrust the ball horizontally or slightly downward toward a nearby teammate, arms extending straight forward from the chest without significant upward lift, the ball travelling in a flat‑low trajectory toward another player

dribble:a basketball player controlling the ball, pushing it toward the ground with one hand and maintaining possession via ball bounce. shoot:a basketball player holding the ball, lifting it upward and releasing it toward a shot attempt with extended arms and wrist flick motion, captured at the moment of ball release

defense:a ball‑free basketball player in a low defensive stance with bent knees and wide‑spread arms, sliding laterally to follow the opponent, actively stopping offensive penetration
pick:a stationary basketball player acting as a physical barrier to screen a defender, arms kept close to the torso or crossed, intentionally obstructing the defender’s path to create space for a teammate (screen)
no_action:a basketball player without the ball standing still on the court, no intentional basketball‑related movement or active game participation
